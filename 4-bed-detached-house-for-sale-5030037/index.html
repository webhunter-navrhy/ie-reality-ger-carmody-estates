About the property

Description property

Situated approximately 4 kilometres west of the popular coastal village of Ballyheigue, this four-bedroom detached bungalow enjoys an exceptional rural coastal setting with uninterrupted and magnificent views across the Atlantic Ocean.

Built in 1975, the property offers generous potential for a purchaser seeking to create a substantial family home in a superb location. Internally, the bungalow requires a full internal fit-out, providing an excellent opportunity to modernise and refurbish the accommodation to suit individual requirements and taste.

The property benefits from its peaceful countryside setting while remaining within easy reach of Ballyheigue and its beautiful sandy beach, local amenities and services. Tralee is approximately 20–22 kilometres away, providing a wider range of shopping, educational, recreational and commercial facilities.

The outstanding feature of this property is undoubtedly its magnificent coastal views extending across the Atlantic Ocean and providing a spectacular backdrop from the house.

With its generous accommodation, detached bungalow layout, excellent renovation potential and truly magnificent sea views, this is a rare opportunity to acquire a property in an attractive coastal location with considerable scope for transformation.
